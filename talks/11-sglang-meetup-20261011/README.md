# SGLang Meetup 2026 - How to Optimize Your SGLang Inference Cluster

**Date:** October 11, 2026
**Event:** AI Infra 开源共创 · 推理引擎专场 SGLang 技术交流 Meetup (Shanghai, co-organized by MetaX 沐曦, SGLang and 模速空间, with Dynamia 密瓜智能 as partner)
**Speaker:** 李孟轩 / Mengxuan Li (@archilitchi) - Dynamia CTO & HAMi Maintainer

## Topic

Are you making good use of your compute? Taking SGLang from bare-metal benchmarks to a production inference cluster that keeps serving stable and efficient under real requests and limited devices:

- Starting from bare-metal SGLang testing and moving to production-grade inference deployment and cluster optimization
- Lowering TTFT (time to first token) and TPOT (time per output token) while raising compute utilization
- Serving inference workloads with fewer devices
- How open-source projects around the SGLang ecosystem (including HAMi GPU sharing, isolation and heterogeneous scheduling) fit into this pipeline
